Wildrune source/reference files. Runtime texture aliases are stored in Assets/Resources/Art and are the artwork directly loaded by GameArt.cs. PNGs from the original source packs that had no Unity asset references were removed to reduce asset-import clutter; remaining source projects, fonts, audio references and license/credit text are kept where available. Replace a runtime alias PNG to change game visuals while keeping its .meta file, or update GameArt.cs to load another texture.
